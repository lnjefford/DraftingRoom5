## Master geometry, files, and optimization

- Master: 1254 × 1254 px square, sRGB PNG with straight alpha. IDs 01–20 use centered black discs. For IDs 21–40, preserve the generated scene-shaped alpha silhouette instead of applying an ellipse mask or filling a disc. Keep their visible art inside a centered 1120 × 1120 px square (at least 67 px transparent margin on each canvas edge); transparent corners and open gaps must remain genuinely transparent. Shapes may extend beyond the old circle's radius while staying inside this square. For IDs 41–50, restore a centered true-black circular backdrop with nominal 1120 px diameter and transparent outer corners.
- For IDs 21–40, keep the action's decisive ivory or gold parts within a centered 910 × 910 px **action safe square**; outer decoration may use the surrounding band, but the scene must survive an inset crop. The old circular gold stroke is optional: if retained, it must be transformed by the action rather than repeated unchanged. At least six of each 21–30 and 31–40 batch must have an unmistakably noncircular outer silhouette at 56 px. For IDs 41–50, keep critical scene details within the black disc and vary the inner scene and gold-frame treatment without losing the shared circular backdrop.
- Make the primary silhouette at least 520 px tall or wide where applicable (about 23 px at 56 px), its main stroke at least 90 px (about 4 px), and any detail necessary to understand the gag at least 45 px (about 2 px). Avoid more than three competing accent groups. The frame may be broken, but its fragments must be thick enough to survive downsampling. These are acceptance targets, not instructions to add stroke outlines to delicate concepts.
- Stable ID and slug: `widget_01_dumbbell` through `widget_50_curtain_call` as spelled below. Save source masters as `docs/design/widget-icon-variants/masters/<id>.png` and batch prompt/provenance as `docs/design/widget-icon-variants/batches/batch-01-10.md` (and analogously for the other ranges). Save optimized runtime derivatives as `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/<id>.webp`; do not use the approved sample PNGs directly as runtime assets. Keep exact IDs in contact sheets and later resource mapping.
- For the runtime derivative, preserve the scene-shaped alpha of IDs 21–40 without a circular mask. IDs 41–50 retain their intended black-disc alpha geometry; use a high-quality edge but do not cut off scene details. Downsample from the master to **256 × 256 px** and encode lossless or high-quality WebP with alpha. Check both size and clarity; target **≤40 KB each and ≤2 MB for all 50**, documenting exceptions if a visibly superior asset needs more. Preserve masters out of APK resources. Record dimensions, format, actual alpha-corner pixel values, byte size, and SHA-256 for each derivative. Never accept an opaque checkerboard as transparency. DR5-055 must remove the prototype provider's `circularArtwork` mask so IDs 21–40 keep their noncircular silhouettes on the home screen.
- At final QA, composite each derivative on light and dark launcher-like backgrounds at **actual 56 × 56 px** with nearest-pixel display (no browser enlargement masquerading as the target size). Also inspect 48 px and a 1 × 1 launcher capture where available. The DR5-048 Pixel Launcher screenshot establishes a feasible footprint, not a promise for every launcher or API version. Art review cannot waive later host/version checks.

## Acceptance rubric for each ten-image batch

Score each item at 56 px, independently, 0 = fail, 1 = needs repair, 2 = pass. Accept only 2 in every column: **single-action recognition** (a viewer can name the scene without the title), **silhouette** (primary shape and action survive), **crop/alpha** (transparent corners, no accidental clipping), **contrast** (readable on light and dark homescreens), **finish** (no stray text, watermark, unwanted artifacts), and **distinctness** (not a recolor or small effect swap of any earlier ID). For IDs 21–40, inspect the outer alpha silhouette without a circular matte and count at least six clearly noncircular results per batch. For IDs 41–50, verify a consistent true-black circular outer background without clipping or forced identical gold borders. Check that every dark silhouette is black rather than navy at actual widget size, while the numeral/frame follows the row's intent rather than a blanket legibility rule. For hidden or fragmented 5s, the scene itself must remain unmistakable and at least one collection identity cue—black field, ivory material, or purposeful gold prop—must connect it to the set. If an item fails, repair or regenerate **that ID**, record the new prompt/provenance, and repeat the 56 px check. Save a labeled 2 × 5 contact sheet that embeds each icon at actual 56 px alongside an enlarged inspection view; record scores and reviewer notes in the corresponding batch manifest. After batch 05, inspect a full 50-image sheet for duplicate compositions, uneven identity, and total APK resource cost.
