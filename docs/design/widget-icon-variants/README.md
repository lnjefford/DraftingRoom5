# DraftingRoom5 widget-icon explorations

The production concept and QA specification for all 50 still looks is in [`CATALOG.md`](CATALOG.md). The completed collection ships with DraftingRoom5 v0.25.0; this page also preserves the four approved direction samples and their original brief.

These four 1254 × 1254 PNGs began as approved direction samples for the one-cell, tap-to-open Android widget. They are **not** launcher-icon replacements or direct runtime widget resources. The existing launcher mark remains unchanged. Their normalized derivatives are part of the shipped set of **50 distinct, icon-readable images** after passing the same small-size QA as the other images.

| File | Visual direction |
| --- | --- |
| `five-dumbbell.png` | The ivory 5 grips a pair of compact dumbbells. |
| `five-flex.png` | The ivory 5 has two small flexing arms. |
| `five-fire.png` | Restrained amber flames rise from the ivory 5. |
| `five-melt.png` | The lower edge of the ivory 5 drips into a small puddle. |

All four were generated with the built-in image-generation tool on 2026-09-12, using the current launcher-mask review (`docs/reviews/DR5-033/icon-mask-review.png`) as a brand reference. For the later variants, `five-dumbbell.png` was also supplied as a sibling style reference. The prompt set held constant: a single square icon composition, dark midnight-navy circular field, large unmistakable ivory numeral **5**, fine warm-gold frame, generous circular safe margins, premium restrained illustration, clear readability at phone-icon size, and no other text, labels, phone mockups, or watermarks. Variant-specific prompts added the dumbbells, flexing arms, flames, or melting effect described above.

The 50-image brief is deliberately broader than the four samples. Treat the 5 and black/ivory/gold identity as recurring anchors across the **collection**, not hard requirements for every individual image. When the concept benefits, the 5 may be obscured, mirrored, fragmented, tipped over, laid down, transformed beyond immediate readability, or temporarily absent; the yellow frame may break, rotate, fold, bend, become a prop, or overlay the scene. A 5 can even be abducted by aliens. Keep these exceptions intentional and visually strong, not random loss of identity. Use strength/training motifs alongside playful surreal or elemental transformations. Avoid near-duplicate recolors or minor effect swaps; each image needs its own instantly legible idea, even when the numeral itself is not legible. Effects may be frozen at their most expressive still frame because the widget rotates whole images rather than playing video. After reviewing batches 01–20, the user clarified that reshaping an inner gold ring is insufficient while every image remains inside the same navy circle, and that any dark silhouette or backing should be black rather than navy. The existing 20 dark backings were corrected to black. Images 21–40 use transparent, scene-shaped outer silhouettes; a design does not need a black backing when its scene works without one. For the final batch, the user requested a return to **black circular backgrounds** for all images 41–50. The batch-specific prompts and QA contract are in `CATALOG.md`.

The shipped provider renders every approved optimized WebP with its original alpha, preserving the noncircular scenes as well as the intentional black discs; DR5-055 removed the prototype circular bitmap mask. Selection is stable within each wall-clock hour and cycles through all 50 images, while Android delivers scheduled widget updates inexactly at an approximately-hourly cadence. App resume and relevant resize, restore, boot, time-change, and package-update events also refresh the current time-selected look. The widget uses no foreground service, wake lock, or per-minute polling. DR5-056 records the final small-size asset review, native API 28/35 tests, launcher evidence, remaining device limitations, and complete release gate.
