# DR5-071 — Accounts and account-management flows

Status: complete in the accumulated, uncommitted Phase 9 checkout. The released app remains v0.26.1.

## Delivered behavior

- Assets exposes the tax-treatment card's **View accounts** entry without moving the table into Accounts.
- Accounts shows no combined total and always presents the approved Employer plans, IRAs, Health savings, Brokerage, Cash, and Properties sections. Linked and manual sources are labeled on each row; Properties and Epic remain explicit later-step placeholders.
- Add asset offers the established connect-institution and manual-account tasks. Plaid's review screen continues to require selection and explicit type, tax treatment, owner, inclusion, and optional manual-account matching before acceptance.
- Account detail shows current value, classification, owner, forecast inclusion, source freshness, holdings availability/content, balance history, and edit actions. Manual accounts receive **Update balance**; healthy linked accounts receive **Refresh linked value**; affected linked accounts receive **Reconnect account**.
- Manual updates append a dated snapshot. Linked accounts never expose a manual balance editor. Local name/classification/owner/inclusion edits append an account revision, and archive requires confirmation while retaining history.
- Provider errors, stale data, and missing item state are derived per provider item and rendered only on the affected account. Reconnect launches update mode for that item ID.

## Navigation and accessibility

Accounts, Add asset, Account detail, Update balance, Balance history, and Edit account are typed `AppRoute` destinations. Their complete stack is primitive-saveable, restores after recreation, and backs out through Account detail → Accounts → Assets without resubmitting writes.

Headings, source labels, row summaries, values, attention state, forecast inclusion, and controls have meaningful merged semantics or explicit descriptions. Every page scrolls for compact, landscape, and large-text layouts. Twenty-eight synthetic account screenshots cover seven representative states at compact, tall, landscape, and 2× text sizes; visual inspection checked list hierarchy, attention/reconnect scoping, readable forms, and absence of clipping in the scrollable layout.
