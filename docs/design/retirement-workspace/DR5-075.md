## Delivered behavior

The native Forecast tab owns one `ForecastCoordinator` while the Forecast route family is composed. It presents modeled success, a 10th/50th/90th-percentile fan chart, an explicit retirement marker, median available-at-retirement buckets, real-dollar annual lifestyle spending, engine/policy identity, the reference-policy disclaimer, and measured tax-funding residuals. The Overview target card now opens Forecast. No Strategy or financial-runway screen was added.

Calculating, needs-data, failed, cancelled, and stale states distinguish the last complete result from current output. A replacement request cancels the old calculation; only a complete generation/revision match can publish. Long horizons request 5,000 instead of 10,000 paths. The screen exposes a stop/restart action and never labels partial output as current.

Forecast settings are one scrollable, restorable form. They cover birth/reference dates, retirement/end ages, lifestyle spending, inflation, expected equity return, volatility scale, filing status, supported state, ACA assumptions, pre-tax/Roth/taxable/HSA contributions, medical spending, existing Social Security/pension amounts and start/end ages, home appreciation, and disposition. One explicit save validates the complete draft and appends one plan revision, which causes one coherent coordinator restart. A clean repository can establish its first plan from the same form. Epic position, growth, tax, sale, and projection fields remain absent and workbook-owned.

Risk details report only observed failure count/timing, unmet spending, retained-access cause categories, and the engine's tax residual. They do not invent a score or claim a market cause. Three focused scenarios show one exact delta: 10% higher spending, a 100-basis-point lower expected equity return, or retirement two years earlier. Preview is isolated, sequential, and reuses the base seed. Apply uses the engine's generation/revision CAS and changes only the displayed typed delta.

Risk and scenario routes persist generation, plan revision, and a bounded scenario identifier. Rotation/process restoration recomputes the base and reopens only if those identifiers still match; stale details fail closed. Ordinary Back returns scenario → risk → Forecast. Settings drafts use saved primitive state.
